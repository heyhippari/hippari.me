This is a field notebook — a place for articles and blog posts about attention, craft, and the habit of paying closer attention to ordinary things.

## Who

Someone who spends most of their time thinking about how systems actually work versus how they're described — in writing, in tools, in the small decisions that shape a piece of work.

Occasional long article. Frequent short post.

## This garden

Articles are long-form and static — documentation, subject notes, checklists, plans. Blog posts are essays of varying lengths. The distinction matters less than the habit of writing things down carefully.

Margin annotations sit beside the paragraph they comment on, rather than interrupting it.
